# tests (owner: claude-fable)

- `Duel.Core.Tests/` xUnit, runs with `dotnet test tests/Duel.Core.Tests`: `TurnFlowTests` and `BattleTests` cover the systems.md §5.5 rules one by one, `ChainTests` cover response windows, chains, triggers and the choice protocol (issue #54) and `MechanicsTests` the modifier registry, equips, control changes, banishing, tokens, counters, once-per-turn effects, life point costs, random discards, Special Summons, Spirits and position locks (issue #55), both with the stub effects in `TestEffects.cs` (one per speed, kind, trigger window and mechanism; no real card), `TierTwoTests` play one scenario per real tier 2 card from `data/cards/` (issue #56) and the heuristic agents through the Beatdown list without its tier 3–4 cards, `TierThreeTests` one scenario per real tier 3 card (issue #57) and the heuristic agents through Rookie Beatdown, Beatdown and Warrior Toolbox, `TierFourTests` the four tier 4 cards (issue #58), every deck in `data/decks/` accepted by the engine and Goat Control played to a winner, `AiTests` play the real tier 1 cards to a winner with the heuristic agents (acceptance for #25) and check the #59 decisions one by one: the response table (a trap that saves a monster or stops a direct attack fires, one that saves nothing stays Set, a counter negates a draw), costs paid low and targets chosen high, optional triggers taken, Traps Set and Normal Spells bluffed only per profile, the evaluator blind to the opponent's face-down monsters, and the heuristic beating random play with the same deck, `FuzzTests` run random agents from 20 fixed seeds, on the vanilla pool, on a pool with every stub response, on a pool with every #55 mechanism, on a pool with every real tier 2 card, on one with every real tier 3 card and on a Goat Control-shaped pool with the tier 4 cards, and assert the §5.7 invariants after every command, `CardLoaderTests` load `data/cards/` and reject bad documents. `Fixtures.cs` builds unshuffled decks whose top cards are scripted and places monsters straight on the field.
- `Data.Tests/` xUnit, runs with `dotnet test tests/Data.Tests`: loads the real `data/` tree through `GameData.Load` (acceptance for #26), checks the decks against `DeckRules`, the duelist chain, shop stock and avatar options, and that every loader rejects malformed documents with a message naming the rule.
- `data_bad/` a deliberately broken `data/` tree; CI runs `tools/validate_data.py --root tests/data_bad` and expects exit 1 with specific messages.
- `scenes/` in-editor diagnostic scenes: SmokeTest.tscn (#24), CharacterTest.tscn (#19), PlayerTest.tscn (#20), CameraFraming.tscn (#21), Profiling.tscn; see `scenes/README.md`
